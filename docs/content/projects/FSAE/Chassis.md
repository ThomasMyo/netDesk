## Approach

- Co-designed the chassis with a focus on driver ergonomics and suspension geometry.
- Ran static finite element analysis on the chassis to find critical weak points and increase rigidity.
- Applied design-for-manufacturing principles to sheet metal and structural tubing, accounting for bend allowances, weld access and machine limits.
- Redesigned the driver cockpit and seat-belt geometry for rules compliance, with fewer, simpler parts that are easier to maintain.
