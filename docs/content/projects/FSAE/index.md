## Chief Engineer (Apr 2025 – Sep 2026)

- Co-led a 60-student multidisciplinary engineering club, overseeing all technical work.
- Led the design and manufacturing effort for the team's first car.
- Coordinated design and manufacturing between the Chassis, Suspension, Powertrain, Aerodynamics and Electrical sub-teams.
- Reviewed proposed designs and manufacturing plans for all critical sub-components.
- Set part-naming standards and assembly conventions, improving sub-team cohesion and club workflow.

## Chassis Lead (Apr 2024 – Apr 2025)

- Led a team of 6 in designing and fabricating the chassis and related sub-components, preventing assembly conflicts and setting packaging limits for Powertrain, Suspension and Aero.
- Mentored junior members in advanced SolidWorks techniques and ANSYS structural analysis.

## Chassis Engineer (Sep 2023 – Apr 2024)

- Co-designed the team's first chassis. See the [chassis write-up](Chassis.md) for details.
